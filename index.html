About P&H Fabrications

P&H Fabrications is a small South Yorkshire fabrication business specialising in bespoke gates and railings.


We work with customers to create made-to-measure metalwork based on their property, measurements and preferred design.


We also take on general fabrication, welding repairs and other custom metalwork projects.
